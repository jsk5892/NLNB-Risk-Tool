How scoring works

Likelihood (1–5)
The average of the answers in a domain. Weaker controls mean higher likelihood. "Not sure" counts as 4, because an unknown control is treated as a weak one.
Impact (1–5)
Comes from the Business Impact Analysis: the highest impact among the critical functions that depend on the domain. Supporting dependencies count at 80%.
Risk score (1–25)
Risk = Likelihood × Impact
1–4.9 Low5–9.9 Moderate10–15.9 High16–25 Critical
Disruption exposure (1–25)
Function impact × weighted likelihood of the domains each bank function relies on.
Overall risk index (0–100)
The average domain risk score, scaled to 100.
