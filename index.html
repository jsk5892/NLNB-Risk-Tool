How scoring works

Probability (1–5): how likely the risk is
The average of the answers to that risk's questions. Weaker controls mean a higher probability. "Not sure" counts as 4, because an unknown control is treated as weak.
Impact (1–5): how serious it would be
60% × inherent severity + 40% × avg. impact of affected bank functions
Inherent severity is set from the case and can be edited in the Risk Register. Function impact comes from the Business Impact tab.
Risk score (1–25) and priority
Score = Probability × Impact
P4 Low · under 6P3 Medium · 6–11.9P2 High · 12–19.9P1 Critical · 20–25
Category and overall rating
The average score of the risks in each category. The overall inherent risk rating is the average of all risk scores, shown out of 100.
